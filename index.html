Step 1

Offer a prayer

Not saved anywhere — no file, no database, no upload. Your prayer stays in this page and disappears when you refresh or close it.

What happens next

  1. A full prayer is composed — opening, your verse, your selected need, your own words, and a closing “Amen”.
  2. Digital signal — the prayer becomes bytes and bits, sampled as a staircase.
  3. Analog waveform — the staircase is smoothed into a flowing analog wave.
  4. The Sacred Heart — the wave travels from your region of the world and the heart glows as it arrives.
  5. Voice — press play and the prayer is spoken in a clear voice, word by word.
