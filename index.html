✝ One Tap. Any Place. The Word Instantly.

A CoreDataLabs-branded NFC card, carried instead of a printed Bible. Tap it on any phone — in the pew, at home, anywhere — and this app opens straight to the search screen. When the pastor says a verse, you're already there.

How it works, physically: the NFC chip (e.g. an NTAG213 sticker or card) is programmed once with this app's URL, e.g. https://yourapp.example/?card=CDL-JESUS-0001&action=enter. No companion app is needed — tapping the card opens that link automatically.

Two ready-to-use tap modes:
· action=open — instantly opens the Bible engine (everyday carry)
· action=enter — same, plus logs a church entry for attendance tracking
